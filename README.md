<h1>osTicket - Ticket Lifecycle: Intake Through Resolution</h1>
This tutorial outlines the lifecycle of a ticket from intake to resolution within the open-source help desk ticketing system osTicket.<br />



<h2>Environments and Technologies Used</h2>

- Microsoft Azure (Virtual Machines/Compute)
- Remote Desktop
- Internet Information Services (IIS)

<h2>Operating Systems Used </h2>

- Windows 10</b> (21H2)

<h2>Ticket Lifecycle Stages</h2>

- Intake
- Assignment and Communication
- Working the Issue
- Resolution

<h2>Lifecycle Stages</h2>

<p>
<img width="1285" height="918" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-25 114331" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/38a6e157-6b5e-4bf7-bb85-b0b266bb7907" />

<p>
The First step in the intake process is to receive the request. The end user contacts you via phone call, email, chat, or ticketing system shown in the example above.
</p>
<br />

<p>
<img width="1275" height="920" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-25 114804" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f49e932c-bc41-4933-8fb6-00447bfd054f" />

</p>
<p>
The next step is to gather information by asking questions like "when did the problem start" or "is anyone else experiencing the issue".
</p>
<br />

<p>
<img width="1262" height="916" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-25 115014" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/10e8cf22-52d5-43d0-9055-123aca4eddbd" />

</p>
<p>
Next update the priority level based on the issue at hand. Here i updated the ticket to Emergency after confirming with the end user that all teller systems were down.
</p>
<br />
<p>
<img width="1282" height="908" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-25 115231" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/982e61e0-bd20-40da-a971-0dedae36f372" />

</p>
<p>
After updating the priority level I then updated the SLA plan to Sev-A, this means that the ticket must be responded too within one hour as the issue is business critical.
</p>
<br />
<p>
<img width="1280" height="912" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-25 115342" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/66f2a569-c25d-45b0-9a61-7e444a4cc900" />

</p>
<p>
I then updated the Help Topic to Business Critical because the entire banking system was down.
</p>
